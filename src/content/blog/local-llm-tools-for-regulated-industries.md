Regulated organizations rely on self-hosted LLMs to process sensitive financial, medical, and legal data within strict compliance boundaries.

### WHY IT MATTERS
Sending confidential client data to external cloud APIs risks severe regulatory fines and intellectual property leaks. Implementing local inference **protects privileged data while maintaining operational compliance**.

### GO DEEPER
- **Healthcare data isolation:** Process patient records locally to comply with **HIPAA** regulations.
- **Financial record privacy:** Prevent exposure of trade secrets and financial models under **SEC** rules.
- **Legal privilege defense:** Ensure attorney-client communications never touch third-party cloud servers.
- **Air-gapped deployment capability:** Run models on isolated internal networks without external access.
- **Custom fine-tuning safety:** Keep proprietary company training data strictly within local perimeter firewalls.
- **Audit-ready logging:** Maintain internal tamper-proof logs to satisfy industry compliance auditors.

### THE BOTTOM LINE
Local LLM deployment is essential for regulated industries to maintain regulatory compliance and data secrecy.
